How it works

How do you set up an iPad video booth?

Four steps, one iPad and a stand. Nobody has to stay with the booth.

  1. Set up once. Install Standby Booth on an iPad (iPadOS 17+), put it on a stand and name the event. With Pro, also set guest prompts, recording length, branding and where clips are saved.
  2. Lock it and walk away. Settings hide behind a triple-tap and a 4-digit PIN. Turn on Guided Access to keep the iPad in the app.
  3. Guests record. Each guest taps once, records, watches it back, then keeps it or tries again. The bright white screen edges light their face.
  4. Collect the clips. After the event, open the Photos library and AirDrop the videos. With Pro, also open Files (On My iPad › Standby Booth) or copy everything to a USB-C drive.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

What is Standby Booth?

Standby Booth is an iPad app that turns an iPad into a self-service video booth and video guestbook for weddings, parties and brand events. Guests walk up, tap once and record a message. It runs fully offline, keeps every clip on the device, and costs a one-time $29.99 for Pro, with no subscription.

How much does Standby Booth cost?

Standby Booth is free to download, and the free version works for a full event with no watermark on the videos. Standby Booth Pro is a one-time $29.99 in-app purchase that unlocks longer clips, up to 4K video, branding and USB-C export. There is no subscription and no per-event fee.

What is the difference between the free version of Standby Booth and Pro?

The free version of Standby Booth records 60-second clips in 720p, allows up to 5 retakes per guest, as configured by the host, saves to the Photos library and shares by AirDrop. Pro adds clips up to 5 minutes with 30-second extensions, up to 4K video, a custom logo, fonts and colors, a looping attract-screen video, guest prompts and a teleprompter, export to a Files folder or USB-C drive, saved event presets, full event stats and a test mode.

Does Standby Booth need Wi-Fi or an internet connection?

No. Standby Booth records and saves every clip on the iPad, so the booth keeps working if the venue Wi-Fi drops or there is no signal at all, and no account is needed. Share clips with AirDrop or export them after the event.

Where does Standby Booth store the videos? Are they uploaded anywhere?

Standby Booth saves videos to the Photos library on your iPad or, with Pro, to a Files folder on the iPad or a USB-C drive. Recordings are never uploaded to Kai Ventures, and clips saved to Photos follow your own iCloud Photos setting. The app sends anonymous usage statistics (Firebase Analytics), never video or event content.

Which devices does Standby Booth work on?

Standby Booth runs on iPad with iPadOS 17 or later. It is not available for iPhone, Mac or Android. The only hardware you need is a compatible iPad and a stand or tripod mount. The screen itself acts as a soft fill light, so most venues need no extra lighting.

How long can each guest record in Standby Booth?

In the free version of Standby Booth each guest records up to 60 seconds. With Pro you set the recording length (up to 5 minutes) and can let guests add up to five 30-second extensions; a 30-second setting keeps wedding messages short and easy to watch.

Can guests change the settings in Standby Booth or mess with the iPad?

No. In Standby Booth, guests only ever see the recording screens. Your settings sit behind a hidden triple-tap and a 4-digit PIN, and you can lock the iPad to the app with Apple’s Guided Access, so the booth runs unattended for the whole event without anyone reaching other apps.

How do I get the videos out of Standby Booth after the event?

With the free version of Standby Booth, open the Photos library on the iPad and AirDrop or share the clips from there. With Pro you can also open Files (On My iPad › Standby Booth), or copy everything to a USB-C drive.

Can I use Standby Booth for a photo booth rental business or brand events?

Yes. Standby Booth’s Terms of Use allow personal and commercial events, including charging clients when you run the booth. Pro adds the tools event businesses ask for: client logos, fonts and colors, guest prompts and a teleprompter, up to 4K recording, USB-C export and saved presets you can load for each client. Pro is a one-time purchase with no per-event or monthly fees.

Can I use Standby Booth Pro on more than one iPad?

Yes, within Apple’s rules. Standby Booth Pro is a one-time purchase tied to the Apple ID that bought it, so you can install Standby Booth on iPads signed in to that Apple ID and restore the purchase, subject to Apple’s Family Sharing and device limits. Apple handles payment, and refunds go through Apple’s Report a Problem page.

How is Standby Booth different from a QR code video guestbook or an audio guestbook phone?

Standby Booth uses one shared iPad at the venue, so guests do not need their own phones, an app or mobile signal, and every clip has the same framing and lighting. Compared with an audio guestbook phone, it records video and needs no extra hardware beyond an iPad and a stand.

Is Standby Booth related to Apple’s StandBy mode?

No. Standby Booth is a separate video booth app from Kai Ventures. It is not part of, or affiliated with, Apple’s StandBy feature, which turns a charging iPhone into a clock and widget display.
